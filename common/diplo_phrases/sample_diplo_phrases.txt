# Oh, dialogue. The bane of my existence, the curse of my hobby, the reason I have a bottle of Ibuprofen on my desk.
# The sample text provided here should give you a decent head start on the mountain you're about to climb, but it obviously doesn't cover every concievable scenario. So, here's a rule of thumb for you:
# propose: I'm playing the game. 'FROM' is the NPC who sent it to me. 'ROOT' is me, the player.
# consider, accept, refuse: I'm playing the game. 'FROM' is me, the player. 'ROOT' is the NPC.
# It is convoluted, and even I still screw it up sometimes. But if you want to have fancy conditions and hyper-specific replies, feel free to look through Hippies, Pirates, and Worker's Union to see how I condition responses to each dialogue check. Be patient, take your time, this is hands-down the hardest part of making a personality mod.
